Common questions

Competitive pathway FAQ

Do you prepare players for ECNL and MLS Next?

Yes. Training is built around the technical speed, decision-making, and physical demands of ECNL and MLS Next — whether your player is trying to make the jump or already competing at that level and wants an edge.

Can you help my player get ready for club tryouts?

Absolutely. Tryout prep sharpens the specific skills evaluators look for — first touch under pressure, 1v1, finishing, and speed of play — plus the confidence to stand out in ID sessions and tryouts.

Do you work with high-school and college-bound players?

Yes. Sessions support high-school varsity players preparing for their season and college-bound athletes refining the game to compete at the next level.
