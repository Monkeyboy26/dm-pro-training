About

Dylan Morrone

I grew up playing competitive soccer in Orange County, California, developing my game with several respected local clubs — including West Coast FC, Strikers FC, and Pateadores Academy.

At 17, I took the next step in my career by moving to Italy to join the Paolo Rossi Academy, training in a highly competitive European environment. While there, an agent invited me to Spain to pursue opportunities with semi-professional clubs.

In Spain, I trained and trialed with multiple teams before signing contracts with CD Tineo in Oviedo and CSD Arzúa in Santiago de Compostela. After roughly three years, a career-ending injury brought my playing days to an unexpected close, and I returned home to Orange County with a new purpose: to pass on the knowledge, experience, and perspective I gained to the next generation of players.

Today, as a professional soccer trainer, my goal is to help young athletes sharpen their technical ability, improve their understanding of the game, build confidence, and develop the habits and mentality needed to reach their full potential — on and off the field.
